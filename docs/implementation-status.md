# Design and interaction review implementation

Scope: the owner's instruction to implement the eight recommendations from the PRD and implementation review. Binding constraints remain in `PRD.md`, sections 16.2, 25, 35, 38 and Appendices B and C. No verification flag was promoted by this implementation.

## Implemented

- The homepage index includes all approved work listings even when a preview is unavailable. Only published screenshot media supply previews. Optimizer URLs and responsive source sets are authored on the server; a missing preview has an honest placeholder (`src/app/page.tsx`, `src/components/work-directory.tsx`).
- Published project covers lead to their case study. Open live remains explicit; RentIt remains an external listing until its publication requirements are met (`src/components/work/project-panel.tsx`, `src/content/release.ts`).
- Figure headings, alt text and captions describe the actual supplied captures. Related existing decisions are linked where their subjects match; captures do not establish unseen behavior (`src/content/figure-stories.ts`, `src/content/projects/*.mdx`, `src/components/work/case-study.tsx`).
- Concept notes support hover across trigger and note, focus, click, Escape and outside dismissal. Touch has a native sheet and inline disclosure fallback. Decision Mode supplies anchored desktop markers and notes, a wide-screen margin rail, touch sheets and inline disclosures; one popup note opens at a time (`src/components/annotations.tsx`, `src/styles/globals.css`).
- Concept anchors follow the recruiter facts and availability in a dedicated band. They no longer compete with the statement, employer, stack and brief in the information band. Actual viewport fit remains unverified (`src/app/page.tsx`, `src/styles/globals.css`).
- Preview swaps use opacity only over 240ms. Notes fade over 150ms. Small labels are at least 12px in the affected controls, note text uses theme-aware contrast tokens, and Experience summaries have an expansion marker (`src/styles/globals.css`, `src/styles/tokens.css`, `design/verify.py`).
- The source reader, viewport resizer and state gallery load on action and have an error boundary. Server-rendered original figures remain available without JavaScript (`src/components/project-experience.tsx`, `src/components/experiences.tsx`, `src/components/work/case-study.tsx`).
- Marginalia sources resolve to supplied assets or evidence from existing decision records. Source markers open a native desktop side sheet or mobile bottom sheet; Show all sources inlines the evidence (`src/components/experiences.tsx`, `src/content/projects/marginalia.mdx`).
- Space Tourism has width presets and a keyboard-operable range control around an explicitly loaded, titled, sandboxed iframe. Annotations reflect the source's 768px background and 1024px desktop/Technology boundaries. Static desktop captures are labeled accurately (`src/components/experiences.tsx`, `docs/truth/space-tourism.md`, section 5).
- FX states are labeled recreations based on the source inventory. The input/result demonstration derives its fixed ratio from the depicted 1,000 USD / 892.54 EUR example; it makes no rates request. Empty-favorites copy was checked against `C:/Users/hp/Desktop/Frontend-mentor/foreign-exchange-checker/starter-code/src/components/Favorites.jsx` (`src/components/experiences.tsx`, `public/figures/foreign-exchange-checker/fx-2026-10-06.png`, `docs/truth/foreign-exchange-checker.md`, section 6).
- Inspect is lazy and available in the frame, mobile menu, palette and disableable I shortcut. It switches to the opposite theme, supports a native View Transition where available, shows authored component/type/token names, computed typography and available visit measurements. Closing restores focus; its mobile panel is inline (`src/components/inspect.tsx`, `src/components/shell.tsx`, `src/components/header-actions.tsx`, `src/lib/measurements.ts`).
- Web Vitals belong to a document load. After client navigation, Inspect does not reuse another route's LCP, INP or CLS as new-route measurements; it offers an ordinary reload link. Resource totals explicitly cover the document visit and exclude its HTML request. Cached or unavailable byte timing is not replaced by an estimate (`src/lib/measurements.ts`, `src/components/inspect.tsx`; [web-vitals documentation](https://github.com/GoogleChrome/web-vitals)).
- Motion preferences combine system reduction, reduced-data/Save-Data, device hints and conservative visitor overrides. Palette controls persist where storage works, with a session fallback otherwise (`src/lib/motion.ts`, `src/lib/motion.test.ts`).
- Shortcut help and command construction are lazy. Decision search excludes unpublished projects and links directly to existing record headings (`src/components/shortcut-sheet.tsx`, `src/components/palette.tsx`, `src/lib/palette.ts`, `src/lib/palette.test.ts`).
- Embeds are restricted to the existing Space Tourism deployment host. The new policy also denies external ancestors and plugins; it is a partial static-compatible policy, not a completed script/style CSP audit (`next.config.ts`, `PRD.md`, section 32).

- RentIt remains unpublished. Editorial illustrations do not satisfy its evidence-figure gate. Its original owner changes and draft content are preserved (`src/content/release.ts`, `src/lib/schemas.ts`, `src/lib/experience.ts`, `src/lib/project-page.test.ts`).
- Actual Space Tourism captures at 375, 768 and 1280px remain missing. Existing desktop captures are not relabeled as those widths or stretched to simulate them. Touch shows the breakpoint explanation and the actual supplied figures (`public/figures/space-tourism/`, `src/components/experiences.tsx`, `docs/truth/space-tourism.md`, section 5).
- A numerical FX history dataset is unavailable. The captured chart remains a figure; no sparkline series was invented. The converter demonstration is a single captured example, not a provider-exported dataset (`public/figures/foreign-exchange-checker/`, `docs/truth/foreign-exchange-checker.md`, section 4).
- Capture pixel density and deployment-to-commit parity remain unverified. Describing visible capture content does not verify either (`src/content/figure-stories.ts`, `docs/truth/space-tourism.md`, `docs/truth/marginalia.md`).
- Current browser behavior, viewport geometry, screen-reader operation, physical devices, iframe rendering and animation frame times remain unverified. The browser-control inventory exposed no available browser. The HTTP smoke test expressly distinguishes these from its results (`docs/review-smoke-results.json`, `scripts/review-smoke.mjs`).
- The owner has not supplied a production metadata host or CV. The build's existing metadataBase warning is retained instead of inventing a host (`src/content/identity.ts`, `docs/phase-1-exit.md`, Appendix A).
