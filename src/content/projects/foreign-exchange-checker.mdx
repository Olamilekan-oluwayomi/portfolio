## My contribution

The design system and assets are supplied by a Frontend Mentor challenge and are credited as such. My work is the state architecture, the request lifecycle with cancellation, the anchor picker behaviour, the history and compare views, and the local persistence for saved pairs and snapshots.

Evidence: docs/truth/foreign-exchange-checker.md:11; docs/phase-1-exit.md, Appendix A item 4, origins.

## Where the page stops short

The audit records what the source does not contain: no currency-specific rounding policy, no loading or error surface inside the chart body, no dialog semantics on the picker, and no selection state on the view buttons. No measured performance figure from the starter template's guide is repeated here.

Evidence: docs/truth/foreign-exchange-checker.md, sections 6 and 7.
