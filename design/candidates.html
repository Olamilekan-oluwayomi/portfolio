Geist Mono is the only one of the three drawn to pair with the body sans beside it (PRD.md:608), and substituting it would break that pairing. That is the reason it is chosen, and it is the weakest of the three reasons here: if the owner prefers either alternative on sight, the change costs one file and one token.

The decision as it stands

No licensing or rendering problem appeared, which is the condition section 23 attaches to using the alternatives. The chosen trio renders correctly, pairs as intended, and measures 95,688 bytes total against a 150 KB budget. So the default stands and no substitution is made. This is reversible: every alternative here is one token and one file away, and the specimen is the place to change it.
