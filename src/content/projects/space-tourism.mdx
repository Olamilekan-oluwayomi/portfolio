## My contribution

The design is supplied by Frontend Mentor and is credited as such. My work is the implementation: the router with route-level lazy imports, the responsive layouts and picture elements, the selection controls, the production build configuration and the motion around selected content.

Evidence: docs/truth/space-tourism.md:11; docs/phase-1-exit.md, Appendix A item 4, origins.

## Where the page stops short

The audit records what the source does not contain: no tablist or tabpanel semantics, no reduced-motion handling, no Escape handling in the mobile menu, no not-found route and no authored no-JavaScript fallback. The README's performance figures have no checked-in audit artifact, so no number from them is repeated here.

Evidence: docs/truth/space-tourism.md, sections 6 and 7.
